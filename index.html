Screening history

Every screening is recorded here — the evidence trail regulators require (e.g. Mexico's 10-year retention). Stored on this device for the prototype; a real backend makes it durable and exportable.

Review queue

Flagged transfers land here for a human analyst to approve or reject — the human-in-the-loop step regulators require. Autonomous systems can't sign off on compliance alone.

Regulatory thresholds & indexed units

LatAm AML thresholds are set in inflation-indexed units, not fixed amounts. These are the exact values the engine uses — with source and date — so every threshold is auditable. USD figures use an indicative FX snapshot and are not exact; units marked snapshot drift over time.
